# Variable Discription

Patient ID-	Unique identifier for each patient (numeric)

Age-	The patient’s age in years (numeric)

Gender-	The patient’s gender (categorical: “Male” or “Female”)

Admission Type-	Whether the admission was “Elective” or “Emergency” (categorical)

Length of Stay-	How many days the patient stayed in hospital during the admission (numeric, days)

Number of Diagnoses	The number of distinct diagnoses assigned during the admission (numeric)

Blood Pressure-	The patient’s systolic blood pressure measured at admission (numeric, mmHg)

Blood Sugar Levels-	The patient’s blood sugar (glucose) level at admission (numeric, mg/dL or mmol/L depending on unit)

Previous Admissions-	The number of previous times the patient was admitted before this admission (numeric)

Readmission-	Whether the patient was readmitted after discharge (“Yes” or “No”) (binary categorical)
